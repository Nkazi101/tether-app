## Corrections made during real-data verification

- Added current-only planning, bounded assessment repair and separate-context model claim review; unsupported optional readings can be withheld without discarding a separately valid comparison.
- Grouped known Wikipedia mirrors and BBC domain/subdomain variants, and rejected substantial copied text as additional source breadth. These checks catch obvious duplication; they do not prove independence.
- Fixed layout/quotation-wrapper false negatives while retaining exact-word passage checks. Historical sequence and interpretation references now stay inside the selected case before the repair step.
- Reassessments are labelled as research work rather than automatically described as new developments in an event.
- Browse cards compact machine-formatted date ranges into readable year labels; the exact period remains available for inspection and in the reader.
- Corrected 41 stored headlines from article titles already in their evidence packets. A real pilot and complete payload comparison verified that every reading/evidence field survived. No model calls were used for those corrections. The first publication attempt exposed a fingerprint omission; failed transactions preserved prior analyses. Adding the actual headline to snapshot inputs resolved it.
- The first automatic poll exposed one full reading replaced by a short feed excerpt. Existing researched reports now receive a full-article check. Canonical source-content comparison preserves unchanged readings across tracking URLs, source IDs, object-key order and routine system-question wording. Changed content and readers’ changed questions still receive reassessment; explicit administrative reassessment bypasses reuse. A live-article check confirmed reuse without publication or model calls. The affected story received one fresh bounded reassessment.
