# Cross-context history and the reader’s incentive

This increment follows the screenshots, the incentive critique, and the earlier attached example treatments. It changes selection and source acquisition as well as presentation. The research basis is recorded in [historical independence](historical-independence-2026-11-04.md); the [original example review](narrative-refresh-2026-11-04.md) distinguishes methodological comparison from an identical-input reproduction.

## The diagnosis

The old engine allowed personal backstory and recent precedent to stand in for recurrence across different lives and circumstances. It also confused a small high-scoring candidate set with a broad historical investigation. At the end of the preceding full refresh, 227 public stories had been reassessed and 66 displayed readings. Minamata was primary for nine stories across seven categories; the 2006 Congress case appeared eight times. Bigger publication counts had not solved selection quality.

The screenshots expose two different defects. Kardashian at the 2019 Emmys is her own recent history, and completing an awards presentation does not explain converting critical hostility into a commercially useful audience. The hacking/Minamata comparison concentrated on observers and disputed evidence while neglecting the actors controlling the hazard and intervention. Delhi/Minamata needed a policy-response aftermath, rather than stopping at chemical identification.

A first stricter pass revealed a third defect. A source-qualified 1972 Reefer Madness revival was available, but a tag-based top-eight shortlist excluded it. The composer substituted generic Picturephone customer uptake. The current abstraction already preserved the distinctive reversal—mockery becoming attention—so the problem was retrieval recall, not lack of historical material or a need for more decorative archetype names.

## The implemented selection contract

- Featured historical parallels require a separate episode and at least a 25-year calendar gap between the current account date and the end of the documented historical case. Fifty years or more and other eras/regions are preferred when fit holds. Twenty-five is an explicit product boundary, not a scientifically established validity threshold. Reporting dates and evidence cutoffs remain dating inputs; historical retrospectives require careful interpretation of the analysed stage.
- The same principal person, a company’s own backstory, and a continuing causal episode do not qualify as the main historical comparison. A durable public institution can recur across distinct episodes and leadership generations, with the continuity explained. Names, aliases, actors outside the chosen mapping and shared participants receive explicit checks. The mechanical full-name guard supplements source-based review; it does not solve every identity ambiguity.
- A comparison must identify a causally relevant driver, the actual leverage and a shared directed constraint/response. Two disconnected facts or a commonplace such as “products need customers” is insufficient. Framing comparisons concern documented tellings; they cannot establish the underlying conduct as fact.
- The current source account stays frozen before history. Satire, attributed claims, hearsay, opinion and reported actions remain separate. Historical outcomes cannot supply missing present actions or turn a settled fact into an open question.
- The consequence of the historical ending must matter to the current fork. Every reading includes a material difference, a credible rival and what history adds beyond the ordinary explanation. These remain automated interpretive judgments, not independently calibrated measurements.

New identity checks refer to actual mapping rows by stable indices. Canonical labels are expanded into a parsed copy before review and storage. The initial guided composition referred to the same actors with concise names and expanded descriptions; literal string equality rejected a legitimate reference. Indexed references repair that representation failure while retaining identity and source requirements.

## Retrieval and source coverage

The default method is now `narrative-research-12`:

1. Type and abstract the current account without introducing history.
2. Filter source-qualified historical records for period and obvious personal continuity.
3. Compare encoded relationships across the eligible library while it is small. A bounded pool of 80 distinct, qualified episodes is the ceiling as it grows; eligible, examined, selected and truncated counts are recorded. Tags order the larger recall pool rather than irreversibly deciding the final eight. Returned IDs and at least two distinct fact references on each side must belong to the supplied records. A legitimate empty shortlist is distinct from an invalid provider response.
4. Search beyond the library even when a local case is plausible. The first pass permits five actual search calls, eight source pages and four proposed episodes. Requested deeper passes permit seven/nine calls and eight/twelve pages. These are bounded investigations, not all-history searches. Reuse counts direct discovery toward missing alternatives; they are not a ban on a strong match.
5. Read and source-encode discovered cases without the current event. Require corroborating origins, a qualified historical foundation, exact source passages and a separate source-only check. Qualified public records remain in the archive even when a particular query cannot use them. One malformed optional candidate does not erase other candidates.
6. Write only from the shortlisted and newly qualified records, preserving the structural rationale and mismatch. Apply the explicit analogy, cross-context, causal-driver and source gates before publication. A separate claim review can withhold the result after a bounded correction.
7. Publish immutable versions through the existing ownership/lease checks and record genuine gaps for expansion.

Conservative aliases are collapsed before ranking and presentation when the same ID or two identical anchored passages and bounded dates establish the same episode. Shared pages or similar titles alone are insufficient. Canonical identity across paraphrases, overlapping scopes and different source packets remains incomplete; archive record counts must not be confused with a proven count of independent historical recurrences.

The public reuse profile scans selected public reporting heads, including pre-policy readings, so demoting them cannot conceal concentration. It batches metadata-only reads, coalesces concurrent loads, and reports incomplete/truncated scans. It excludes private requests and walkthroughs. [Reuse-profile details](historical-reuse-profile.md), [ranking checks](structural-candidate-ranking-checks.md).

## Source acquisition repairs

The history reader had silently omitted substantive prose placed in older h5/h6 elements. A Snow transcription yielded only 241 characters of citations; the corrected reader retained about 11,859 characters of actual account. Short author credits and timeline headings are now preserved. A 23-character Gerald Donaldson credit had also been lost to the paragraph-length filter.

The PDF reader inserted a space after every font run, splitting words such as “film” and “office.” It now follows the text-item joining behaviour used by the installed unpdf library. A generated PDF with adjacent font fragments tests the defect end to end. Literal quotation requirements were preserved.

Source admission also had a sequencing problem: cheap first-pass origin labels could veto an account before the separate reviewer considered them. The source-only reviewer can now correct mistaken triage with a literal basis. Credited expert history does not need a new archival discovery to qualify; prestige or ordinary recycled summaries are still insufficient. Case titles and reader-visible historical metadata are included in source checking.

Eight explicitly researched leads yielded six source-qualified additions or extensions across entertainment, health, politics and environmental response. Others remained withheld. The fuller Prost account required contemporaneous 1991 reporting alongside corroborating career/1993 records. Its documented sabbatical and championship return were appended under the existing canonical episode, with the discovery alias retained as reference-only. An admitted lead is not automatically a good current-event match.

## The experience

The page starts from a headline-only reader: a brief source recap and prominent original link establish what happened. The historical result sits beside a concrete current question; then the aligned facts show the shared pressure. The consequential difference and rival remain readable. Historical beats carry the unfamiliar information; present beats are reminders.

Supported dynamics name participants and show observable evidence with a counter-reading. Proper narrative terms retain hover, focus, tap and Escape definitions in comparison prose. The framework is episode-scoped and Jung/Campbell-inspired; vocabulary and lineage do not establish psychological truth.

Evidence labels count observations by epistemic tier and actual source accounts. They do not describe every sourced claim as independently verified. The view has one Follow action, including Reading, Watch and Sources. Decorative dots, thick callouts and duplicate labels were reduced. Source passages and truly secondary material use expandable details.

The hero and featured comparison set use distinct historical episodes and seek category variety among qualified readings. The broader candidate pool is separate from chronological pagination. The full reporting stream stays available; selected primaries are not silently exchanged to manufacture variety.

The attachment is inspiration, not a specification to implement indiscriminately. No inferred political profiles, invented matching-marker counts, public accuracy rate or compulsory new archetype labels were added. Two participants can legitimately perform the same role.

## Validation and limits

The guided Kardashian regression recovered Reefer Madness from outside the former top eight and published a sourced reading after identity-reference repair. This is an informed regression check, not a held-out analogy benchmark or independent human validation. Software tests check date boundaries, identity/continuity exclusions, immutable inputs, reference integrity, alias conservatism, source extraction, access controls and feedback behaviour. Usefulness votes remain private, deletable and specific to each case/version; they do not adjudicate factual truth.

Following tracks comparison versions. Cross-article event identity, automated resolution of watch questions, independent accuracy measurement, representative multilingual coverage, and forecast advantage remain unfinished. A small canon plus bounded web research can still miss a closer case. Current-source perspective gaps remain visible instead of being filled with invented symmetry.

Ordinary daily model allowances remain unchanged. The controlled refresh has a separate cumulative lifetime ledger; previous usage was retained across method changes. Reservations are conservative UTF-8-byte-plus-output units, distinct from observed provider token usage. Aborted usage is unknown. This one-off refresh does not establish that every future headline receives immediate deep research under the ordinary allowance.
