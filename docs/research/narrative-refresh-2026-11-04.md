# Narrative comparisons: example review and implementation

The method-10 batch described here completed all 227 public reporting targets, producing 66 contextual readings and 47 usable historical encodings at that checkpoint. The later [cross-context implementation](cross-context-implementation-2026-11-05.md) supersedes its retrieval limits, historical-distance rules and final data census. These figures describe a completed earlier pass, not the current website totals.

The attached treatments describe a broader product than the previous action-sequence matcher implemented. The model was visible in the glossary but did not govern most of the comparisons. Four public parallels were a pipeline failure, not evidence that only four historical comparisons exist.

## What the examples establish

The original articles, links and complete source packets were not attached; the attachments contain their analytical treatments. Consequently this is a comparison of methods and documented claims, not an independent reproduction from identical inputs. The examples also inform the new historical leads. Success on those leads is a guided acceptance check, not a held-out retrieval benchmark.

## Why four was a warning

The baseline contained 227 public reporting heads and four published comparisons. The current-method archive was only 15 usable encodings, despite many more stored case versions. Most feed heads had older research or none. Ordinary background research had a 60-call daily ceiling and a substantial deferred queue. The old pilot’s allowance was exhausted; queuing work did not refresh it.

The action matcher required three distinct predicates, ordered moments and consistent mappings. Those checks still have value for the narrow assertion that two action sequences align. Applying them as the admission test for every historical reading excluded elections, satire, ongoing institutional arrangements and technological transitions.

Re-encoding 45 older candidate cases did not turn them into 45 qualified references. Only three survived that pass, two of them very recent cases unsuitable as historical references for the current batch. Failures included unsupported licensing claims, a contradictory claim that an apology never happened, source-origin misclassification, dates outside a bounded case, missing source access and unsupported historical mechanisms. Larger row counts would have hidden these problems.

## The implemented method

1. Read and type the current account first: event, process, narrative artifact, mixture or insufficient material. Preserve reported facts, attributed claims, hearsay, opinion and satire as different tiers.
2. Freeze a source-anchored abstraction before retrieving history. Strip identifying details from the search abstraction while preserving power, dependence, conflict, stakes and constraints. Cast figures and interpret dynamics with evidence and qualifications. Empty dynamics are valid.
3. Retrieve source-encoded historical records through mechanisms, roles, dynamics and stage. The comparator checks actual sourced correspondences. Tags are candidate leads, not proof. The previous typed action matcher remains available for its narrower kind of comparison.
4. Search beyond the library for every new eligible assessment when web research is configured. The current corpus has no demonstrated broad recall, so a plausible local candidate must not prevent discovery of a closer one. The first pass allows three search calls and up to six readable pages; requested deeper passes allow five or seven calls and up to eight pages. Record the actual provider search trace. Read the discovered sources, establish independent origins, encode the historical case without the current event and check it in a separate source-only pass. A proposal from model memory cannot bypass this path.
5. Compare at least two distinct sourced facts on each side. Name whether this contextual match concerns a mechanism, framing or a power relationship. Complete ordered action-sequence claims use the separate typed matcher. Require a consequential break, a credible rival reading, an ordinary explanation and a specific account of what history adds.
6. Run a separate automated claim check against the supplied current and historical source texts. A weak additional case can be omitted. An unsupported central comparison is withheld. Additional requested passes must try new contexts and countercases within explicit bounds; there are no unlimited retries until approval.
7. Source-record corrections are bounded: one explicit correction against recorded issues, followed by another check. No new source, invented fact or current-event target can be introduced during that correction.
8. Publish immutable versions atomically. Record open coverage gaps. Grow the archive from verified retrieval, weekly demand clusters, reader source flags and periodic source rechecks.

There is no claim to search all recorded history or establish a universal collective unconscious. A fixed Jung/Campbell-inspired vocabulary gives the readings a consistent language. Historical evidence establishes the particular comparison; the vocabulary does not validate it.

## Reading experience

The article and image lead. A concise insight gives the payoff. The comparison puts the documented earlier result beside today’s open question, then presents current and historical facts alongside one another. The consequential difference explains what could change the path. The outcome and the question are connected parts of the reading, not optional appendices. Source passages, account typing, role qualifications and theory sit in expandable sections. Supported dynamics are visible with their named participants, a plausible alternative and expandable evidence for each required part of Projection, Inflation or Compensation. Proper narrative terms have definitions where they occur in comparison prose, accessible by hover, focus and tap. Article quotations remain intact.

The cast includes Hero, Apprentice, Scapegoat, Tyrant, Trickster, Wise Elder, Ruler, Challenger, Guardian, Mediator, Collective and Magician. Projection, Inflation and Compensation remain distinct dynamics, with source requirements and alternative explanations. The stage vocabulary is Campbell-inspired rather than attributed to Jung. These are roles and interpretations, not personality diagnoses.

Each displayed historical case can receive its own private, version-specific upvote/downvote and optional explanation. Votes measure usefulness and identify problems; they are not accuracy votes. Exposure is measured in the browser instead of being hard-coded as having occurred.

## Refresh and evidence

The full refresh has its own durable, bounded ledger and request/response receipts under `audit/lens-refresh-9`. It does not reset the exhausted earlier pilot or silently alter ordinary daily limits. Existing records are retained for audit; deletion is not necessary to reassess the public feed. Each source is refetched where accessible, and each public head receives a new-method result or an explicitly recorded retry/access failure.

A successful technical check demonstrates pipeline behaviour, access controls and schema consistency. It does not demonstrate independently measured historical accuracy. The previous 0/14 cross-case leave-one-out result remains an unresolved historical retrieval benchmark; synthetic fixtures are not a substitute. ## The reasoning behind the latest experience changes

The earlier design followed the analysis procedure: classify, explain, qualify, then eventually reveal a historical continuation. The reader's purpose runs the other way. They want to understand the pressure, see what that pressure produced before, and have a concrete question that could change their expectation today. Hiding the result made the main reason to return hard to find.

That diagnosis applies to source encoding, generation, feed previews, the comparison opening, visible dynamics, consequential differences, following and feedback. Historical records now contain sourced outcomes and an as-of period. The reader sees the historical result and current fork together. The feed previews the result where available. The account of Projection, Inflation or Compensation needs a named actor and the specific evidence for its definition. Applying equal evidential tests does not require equal blame, and an external vantage point does not grant access to private motives.

The first actual refresh exposed semantic failures that schema checks missed: conventional praise was being called Inflation, and staff containing a cyber incident were being called Compensation. Dynamic-specific observations and a separate current-source annotation pass now remove such optional readings before any historical case is introduced. Their removal need not discard a useful factual comparison.

## Operational failures found and corrected

- The automatic public research queue held 246 older queued jobs and four running jobs against a 250-job owner limit. This left only four admission slots. A service-only, ownership-checked supersession function retired 219 obsolete queued jobs for the selected public situations; private requests, current-method jobs and live leases were preserved. Method upgrades now perform that cleanup automatically. Feed research keys also include the research method, so a previous method's completed job cannot suppress fresh work.
- The current article extractor kept the opening 6,000 characters and excluded short paragraphs. It could lose a rebuttal or consequential ending while preserving the accusation. It now retains short paragraphs and up to 12,000 characters; longer text retains opening and ending as explicitly noncontiguous segments. Quotes cannot bridge the omission. Image captions are separately attributed, not mistaken for the article lead or independent event evidence. PDF extraction explicitly remains bounded to the first 40 pages.
- Default Node 26 HTTP/2 model traffic completed in a serial pattern despite concurrent tasks. Two tiny controlled requests took 2.52 and 4.61 seconds on the default transport; the explicit HTTP/1.1 pool completed both in 2.43 seconds. The model client now uses that bounded, separate pool. This is an observed transport diagnosis, not a provider-wide performance guarantee. A stopped controlled run preserved 18 completed current-source frames in its own job checkpoints. Aborted request usage is unknown and was not treated as refunded.
- Aborted provider requests had been mistaken for research outcomes. They now defer safely, preserve published readings and reuse matching source checkpoints. Deferral messages no longer claim every retry is waiting for the daily allowance.
- A malformed optional historical lead could abort an investigation even when other qualified candidates existed. Candidate-level representation failures are now recorded individually. A successful source-only historical encoding is retained for future public retrieval even when it is not selected for the current reading; qualification of history and fit to a current event are separate decisions.
- Stored historical summaries and excerpts were not always sufficient to establish their encoded facts or endings. Fresh source acquisition and source-only re-encoding withheld records that lacked support. A guided verifier regression also caught a misleading Magna Carta chronology in a National Archives summary. A prestigious source can contain an error; agreement between automated passes is not independent historical validation. The record was narrowed/corrected rather than allowing the unsupported chronology to become a precedent. The attempted additional Parliament source was blocked programmatically and is not counted as ingested corroboration.

Source for the transport design: [Node fetch custom dispatchers](https://nodejs.org/api/globals.html#fetch) and [Undici connection pools](https://github.com/nodejs/undici/blob/main/docs/docs/api/Pool.md). The measured diagnostic receipts are in the refresh audit directory.

## Remaining product limits

Following tracks versions of a comparison. It does not yet combine all subsequent articles about the same developing event into a verified narrative thread or automatically resolve each watch question. Current accounts can still omit an opponent's perspective; the model must expose that limitation rather than manufacture symmetry. A larger corpus, multilingual source coverage, held-out analogy recovery and measured reader comprehension remain necessary work. Daily operating allowances still limit throughput; the separately budgeted full refresh is not a promise that every future headline will receive immediate deep research.
