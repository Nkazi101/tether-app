# Tether: the reader insight contract

3 November 2026 · follows the user's fourteen requested insights

**Product direction:** recognise the human drama taking shape, understand how people participate in it, examine earlier expressions, and notice where a different response remains possible. Both **enacted behaviour** and **stories told about that behaviour** matter. A media frame is one layer of this product, not its entire subject.

**Implementation status:** a first structured-lens implementation now exists in the analysis pipeline and reader. It is not an independently validated detector of archetypes, unconscious motives or historical outcomes. Existing immutable analyses are not retroactively upgraded. See [the research synthesis](archetypal-narratives-and-validation.md) for the theory, implementation audit and evaluation design, and [verification](../design/narrative-lenses-verification.md) for checks actually performed.

## The form factor

The unit people browse is **one interesting reading of a real story**, with a particular historical expression that helps test it. It should feel like receiving a revealing perspective from an informed friend. The reader should not have to select an archetype, study a taxonomy or operate a research dashboard before finding value.

Keep the established photographic, scrollable discovery experience and category breadth. A new lens adds a short title and one useful insight to a card. The reader couples the article image with that reading and names its historical expression. Three destinations remain: **The reading**, **What to watch**, and **Sources & limits**. An input for a link or description remains an optional entry point.

In The reading, show the observations behind the interpretation, attributed roles and actual power when known, a supported stage, a competing reading, and the consequential difference. Exact source passages open beside each claim. Optional deeper signals contain their observation, interpretation, rival explanation and limit in one place. They do not become five mandatory sections. A second distinct lens uses an explicit selector rather than nesting an entire reading inside another disclosure. Each lens already carries a competing reading; a second full lens is not mandatory.

A lens has a short editorial budget: title at most 90 characters, main insight at most 360, and individual supporting claims at most 500. These are ceilings, not writing targets. Prefer two roles and zero or one deeper signal. Empty evidence does not get filled with dramatic prose. Existing analyses use their original historical summary and optional legacy interpretation until a new assessment earns the new representation.

This keeps the earlier design rationale: ordinary readers control the pace, useful detail has clear labels, and relevant photography supplies context. The choice is informed by research on progressive disclosure and news formats; it still needs direct comprehension testing with Tether readers. [NN/g, progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/); [Reuters Institute, 2025 executive summary](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/dnr-executive-summary).

Power should be analysed relationally rather than assigned as a blanket trait. Emerson's framework is useful here; Bachrach and Baratz additionally draw attention to which issues reach decision-making at all. Neither permits inferring a secret agenda merely from a missing sentence. [Emerson, 1962](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Power/Emerson_1962_Power-dependence_relations.pdf); [Bachrach and Baratz, 1962](https://ftp.columbia.edu/itc/sipa/U6800/readings-sm/bachrach.pdf).

There is empirical reason to take images seriously: Slovic and colleagues examined attention and donations around a particular iconic humanitarian photograph, finding a short-lived response. That bounded case does not prove that every recurring image has the same psychological meaning or effect. The paper also has a published correction, which should accompany any detailed reuse. [Author university record and abstract](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/publications/iconic-photographs-and-the-ebb-and-flow-of-empathic-response-to-h/); [correction record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28193883/).

The pendulum has a recognisable Jungian analogue in enantiodromia. Treat that as an interpretive concept. Wlezien's thermostatic policy research supplies a specific, testable form of feedback between preferences and spending; it cannot validate a universal cultural swing. [ARAS concordance of Jung's usage](https://aras.org/concordance/enantiodromia); [Wlezien, 1995](https://developmentengagementlab.org/storage/the-public-as-a-thermostat-dynamics-for-preferences-for-spending-1581607733.pdf).

For fluency, Gentner, Loewenstein and Thompson found benefits of comparing cases for schema abstraction and transfer in negotiation studies. This supports testing explicit comparisons in Tether, not assuming that news browsing automatically produces expertise. Reader evaluation should include appropriately rejecting an attractive analogy. [Gentner et al., 2003](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/GentnerLoewensteinThompson03.pdf).

## The implemented analysis sequence

1. Read the current source material, preserving source identity and timing. Feed excerpts remain identified when full text is unavailable; reader descriptions remain unverified accounts.
2. Make a bounded, tool-free planning call using **current evidence only**. Record up to two provisional human-drama hypotheses, supporting passages, narrative queries, an independent material query and a countercandidate query. No hypothesis is also a valid outcome. The planner is not given the stored historical case or its ending, although a pretrained model may already know history.
3. Validate the plan's current-source passages. Then search using the hypotheses, material relationships and alternatives. If planning fails, retain the independent historical-search path rather than treat the failure as evidence that no archetype exists. The search prompt asks for each retrieval path; the recorded query trace shows what actually ran. There is not yet a separate automated audit proving every requested query lane was executed.
4. Read bounded historical pages and compare specific episodes. The historical narrative must be grounded in the selected episode, not a different candidate with a familiar archetype. Let history revise the preliminary interpretation.
5. Validate factual comparison gates and optional lens provenance separately. Reject invalid quotations, unresolved references, wrong-side evidence, absent rivals, and unsupported historical boundaries. Projection and grandiosity need a separate observation of conduct or constraints. An invalid optional lens is withheld without discarding a separately valid historical comparison.
6. Publish a versioned, scoped interpretation with explicit uncertainty. Legacy interpretations do not silently become new lenses. A challenging/retracted reader observation suspends the earlier structured lenses pending reassessment; the previous version remains available.

The representation is carried in the existing immutable comparison payload. No new database tables, tracking collection or vector service are needed for this increment. The research version changes; the general ingestion engine version remains unchanged to avoid blanket reprocessing of the existing feed.

Planning adds up to one model call per investigation. It uses the existing shared call/token reservation limits and bounded output/timeouts. Search and final assessment retain separate failure handling. The subsequent live data refresh added one bounded assessment repair and a separate model claim review, so successful research can use up to five calls; with fixed daily caps, fewer investigations may finish. The original implementation was verified with fixtures. The subsequent [public data refresh](../public-narrative-refresh.md) records real-provider trials and the defects they exposed. Model-assisted checking still does not establish historical or psychological accuracy; independent evaluation remains outstanding.

The current broader search still has a small bounded first pass (the prompt requests at most four searches; source retrieval selects at most eight pages). This is a starting investigation, not coverage of all history. More pages alone will not solve interpretive validity. Subsequent work needs multilingual historical source coverage, indexed narrative/sequence evidence, countercases, source-dependency tracking and a benchmark-led escalation policy. A limited result must continue to say that research is incomplete.

## Acceptance and rollout

The software gate checks source access and representation. The **meaning gate** still needs independent review: did the passage support the claim, did the actor actually adopt that role, was the relevant context omitted, is the historical case useful, and does the alternative change the analysis? Model self-agreement is insufficient.

Use the pilot evaluation proposed in the synthesis before treating these as broadly accurate classifications. Compare narrative-guided, material-only and combined retrieval under matched budgets. Include same-archetype hard negatives, unjustified blame, satire/quotation, genuine hazards, positive cooperation, cross-cultural cases and incomplete evidence. Report disagreement and false-positive rates, not just examples that sound insightful.

For form-factor research, recruit ordinary readers and news avoiders. After a short unassisted encounter ask them to explain the event, the underlying reading, who assigns a role, what power is documented, one historical correspondence, the consequential difference and a plausible competing reading. Measure time to useful insight, wrong turns, perceived effort and false certainty. Test one strong lens against an all-features layout on the same content. Optional reader reflection must add agency without making every visit a lesson.

Successful retention should come from a reader gaining a transferable way to see. Returns and saves can help monitor use; they cannot establish that the product has taught that skill.
