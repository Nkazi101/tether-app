# Design from the reader’s purpose

4 November 2026

The reader is trying to understand a live situation and develop an expectation they can revise. Completing an analysis pipeline is not that reader’s goal. The previous experience gave verification, qualifications and the ontology more prominence than the useful question: what happened when this pressure appeared before, and what could change its course now?

This explains several apparently separate defects: outcomes hidden in an optional continuation, dynamics hidden in a drawer, cards advertising a comparison without revealing its practical value, generic watch questions, and an article-shaped unit that does not automatically assemble every subsequent report into a developing event.

## The experience contract

- The feed starts with sourced reporting and an image. A clearly labelled reading supplies an interesting perspective. Where a sourced historical result exists, its preview makes the reason to open the comparison concrete.
- The comparison introduces the particular earlier case and its recorded result alongside the current question. Side-by-side facts make the overlap inspectable. The consequential difference is a fork in the reasoning, not a perfunctory disclaimer.
- A historical result is a documented result as of a stated period, not a permanent resolution or a frequency claim. An unresolved historical situation stays unresolved. A missing ending is never filled from model memory.
- A visible dynamic belongs to named participants and separate observations of language and conduct. Projection, Inflation and Compensation are proper terms with nearby definitions. They must explain a tension rather than decorate a paragraph. Ordinary tribute language is not exceptional self-identification; reciprocal criticism alone is not Projection.
- Both sides receive the same evidential procedure. This does not require equal blame or equal findings. An external vantage point helps organise competing accounts; it does not provide access to private motives. Missing perspectives remain visible in the evidence details.
- A question to follow discriminates between plausible paths. Its explanation tells the reader what changes if the evidence goes one way, rather than narrating an internal validation procedure. A known ending cannot be passed off as a future question.
- Source qualifications and theory remain accessible where relevant. They must not consume the first screen or replace a concrete insight. Feedback is attached to each actual parallel and version.

The contract applies to generation, source records, retrieval, comparison, preview cards, the reader, watch view and quality checks. Fixing one presentation without fixing its data contract would leave the same defect elsewhere.

## Implemented in this increment

Historical records can now carry a source-anchored outcome and as-of period. New comparisons require an explicit actor mapping, shared constraint and shared response before the prose can be published. The current extraction has named dynamic participants and separate rhetoric/conduct evidence. The preview, comparison overview, historical-result evidence, consequential difference, visible dynamic section and watch copy use that common representation. Earlier analyses remain immutable; their existence does not silently grant the new comparison badge.

The first real refresh also found that quote occurrence was insufficient. Some older canonical summaries included dates or conclusions not supported by their stored exact excerpts. The historical library is being fetched and encoded again from actual readable sources. A source-access failure does not prove an old account false; it also cannot grant a newly checked encoding. Such entries remain discovery leads until the new source path succeeds.

## What this does not establish

This is a product hypothesis informed by the reader’s stated purpose, not a completed usability study or a demonstrated retention gain. Automated source checks and upvotes do not measure independent historical accuracy.

Following currently tracks versions of the selected comparison. It does not yet assemble all newly published articles about a developing event into one verified narrative thread. That event-continuity gap matters to the anticipation incentive and must not be obscured by promising that every new headline will update a followed question. The existing immutable versions, source dates and watch questions are useful foundations; cross-article event identity and verified question-resolution updates remain a separate architectural requirement.
